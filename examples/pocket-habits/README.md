# Pocket Habits

Pocket Habits is a tiny habit tracker for people who want streaks without the spreadsheet.
Add a habit in two seconds, check in with one tap, and watch your weekly heatmap fill up.

## Features
- One-tap daily check-ins with streak counters
- Weekly heatmap and completion stats
- Works offline, data stays in your browser
- Available on iPhone and Android (installable PWA, coming to the App Store and Google Play)
